## Known limitations

- All final RC5 package formats are built and independently verified. Full
  owner/live-provider acceptance remains pending; scoped automated and guest
  evidence is detailed in the RC5 qualification report.
- Widget timing is sensitive to host contention. Unpinned runs exceeded the
  limit in both old and new binaries; a controlled CPU-affinity pair passes
  the unchanged limits. The exact final RC5 daemon also passes in the recorded environment.
- First-use CalDAV this-and-future and cached floating upgrades pass development
  verification, including positive, negative, offline and interrupted paths.
  The exact final RC5 backend bytes also pass these checks.
- CalDAV this-and-future RSVP is deliberately unavailable: Nextcloud retains
  the attendee's ranged change but delivers only one occurrence to the
  organizer. Storage capability proof cannot establish scheduling support.
  Series and occurrence RSVP remain available on qualified scheduling servers.
- Dedicated Google/Fastmail accounts were offered by the owner; authentication
  and all applicable exact-candidate workflows remain pending.
- Real Omarchy guest and exact Flatpak checks now pass their recorded scopes.
  Remaining owner, physical mixed-display and suspend/resume checks are separate.
- Widget qualification/publication and AUR/Flathub distribution are independent
  of a GitHub app release. A Flatpak bundle does not imply a Flathub listing.
